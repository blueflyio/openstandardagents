## Notes on source quality and usage

- **Official protocol/framework docs** are treated as authoritative for definitions and architecture claims.  
- **Academic papers and university indexes** are prioritized for risk/governance evidence.  
- **Vendor reports/blogs** are included for practical implementation data, with caveats where claims are self-reported or not externally audited.  
- **Community blogs (for example Dev.to)** are used as tactical practitioner guidance and cross-checked with stronger sources where possible.
