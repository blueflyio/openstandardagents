## Source quality notes

- University/research/standards sources ([T07]-[T12], [T15]-[T21], [T47]-[T50]) are treated as **higher-trust** for factual claims.
- Vendor and project pages ([T01]-[T06], [T22]-[T46]) are used for **product capabilities/adoption claims**, with explicit caveats where needed.
- Blog and community sources ([T51]-[T53]) are used for **engineering patterns, case studies, and heuristics**, not as sole evidence for normative claims.
- Some HBR content ([T13]) is partly paywalled; only publicly visible summary material is cited directly.
